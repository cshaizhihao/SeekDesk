Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a landscape editorial cover illustration for the empty reading pane of SeekDesk, a native macOS desktop forum for server and network enthusiasts.
Primary request: an original, tactile printmaking illustration of a nocturnal undersea fibre-optic network, as if a small sheet of an oceanographic atlas had become the identity of the application. Show a handful of fine topographic bathymetry contours, three clearly visible tiny warm copper connection nodes, and one deliberate fibre route arcing between them. The shapes can suggest a folded seabed or a distant headland; keep the visual language abstract and crafted.
Composition: extremely wide-feeling 3:2 landscape. Make the strongest recognizable topology occupy the central horizontal band, with a wide margin at top and bottom, so a shallow 2.45:1 center crop still reads. Spread the contours and nodes across the center with open dark space between them; avoid a central badge, large computer or isolated icon. The illustration will be placed above native text and does not need any text inside it.
Medium and palette: finely engraved ink, subtle matte paper grain, midnight blue ink #142436, deep desaturated slate #23394C, glacial blue #7299B2 for thin lines, restrained copper #D3A36E for a few points. Crisp enough to read at thumbnail size; premium technical publishing mood. No neon, no glow floods, no gradients that look like glass, no generic cyberpunk, no random decorative circuitry. Absolutely no words, typography, letters, numerals, labels, logos, UI chrome, borders or watermark.
